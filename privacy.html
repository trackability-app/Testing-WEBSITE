Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 3 October 2026

Trackability is an independently operated study, productivity and personal-tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it may be stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

Depending on the features used, Trackability may collect email address, exam, course or academic category, and exam date/year where applicable.

User-entered information

You may enter tasks and schedules, subjects and syllabus information, study hours, focus/Pomodoro sessions, revision records, MCQ information, Grand Test information and scores, mistake-book entries, statistics/goals/progress, gym/workouts, sleep, body weight, protein, calories/nutrition and other information you voluntarily choose to enter.

Local and cloud storage

Some information may be stored locally or in browser storage. Authenticated account information may sync to cloud infrastructure. Trackability uses Supabase for authentication and cloud storage.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the service and requested features.
  • Authenticate accounts and synchronize supported data.
  • Communicate about support, service and important changes.
  • Maintain security, reliability and performance.
  • Understand usage patterns and improve features.
  • Use aggregated, anonymized or de-identified information for product development, statistical analysis, research and general pattern analysis.

3. Privacy and Disclosure

Trackability does not sell personal information. We do not intend to publicly disclose your name, email, account credentials, individual study records, health/nutrition records or exam records except where necessary to provide the service, required by law, necessary for security, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Providers

Third-party providers may process limited information as necessary for authentication, cloud infrastructure, hosting, email, payments and related service functions. Supabase is used for authentication/cloud storage. Razorpay may process payment information. Trackability does not intend to store CVV, UPI PIN or internet-banking passwords. Transaction/order/subscription status and dates may be retained.

5. Aggregated and Research Data

Trackability may use aggregated, anonymized or de-identified data for future product development, statistical analysis, research and understanding general study/productivity patterns. Such analysis is intended not to expose individual identity.

6. Trials, Payments and Subscriptions

Trackability may offer a 14-day trial or other promotional period. Eligibility, pricing and subscription details will be shown before purchase and may change. Payments may be processed by Razorpay under its applicable terms.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts and synchronization, provide support, maintain transaction records, address fraud or disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Deletion

You may request account/data deletion by emailing trackability.app@gmail.com. We may verify account ownership. Certain information may need to be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for legitimate records.

10. Health, Fitness and Nutrition

Gym, sleep, weight, protein and calorie features are tracking tools only. They do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or professional medical advice.

11. Examination Disclaimer

Trackability is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NEET-PG, NEET-UG, INI-CET, JEE, UPSC, USMLE or any examination authority unless expressly stated. Trackability does not guarantee marks, rank, percentile, admission, college, qualification or career outcomes.

12. Children

Trackability is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, features, providers or legal requirements change. The updated date will be shown at the top.
