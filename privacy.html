Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Overview

Trackability is a productivity and tracking application. This policy explains the categories of information that may be stored when you use an account and the purposes for which the application uses that information.

2. Information you provide

  • Account information such as email address and profile details.
  • App records you choose to create, such as tasks, focus sessions, subjects, chapters, MCQ records, Grand Tests, mistakes, workouts, sleep, reviews and nutrition logs.
  • Messages you send to support.

3. How data is used

Data is used to provide the application's tracking, analytics, account and synchronization features, to restore your workspace across supported devices, and to respond to support requests.

4. Cloud infrastructure

The current implementation uses Supabase authentication and database-backed cloud state. Your account data is associated with your authenticated user account so that cloud data can be isolated per user.

5. Local storage

The application can keep app state locally on the device and use local state during guest use or before cloud synchronization.

6. Support

For privacy questions or data requests, email trackability.app@gmail.com.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as Trackability's architecture and features evolve. Publish the final version with your legal/business identity and applicable jurisdiction-specific requirements.

This is a product-ready draft, not legal advice. Review it with an appropriate legal professional before treating it as the final policy.
